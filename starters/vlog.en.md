# Guideline: vlog

## Brand style

- Tone: casual, friendly, personal. Talk to the viewer like a friend.
- Pacing: shots of 1.5-3 seconds. Cut out pauses, "um"s and dead air.
- Transitions: mostly hard cuts; an occasional quick slide or zoom to mark a new place or topic.
- Captions: lower middle of the frame, large enough to read on a phone, shown word by word in sync with speech.
- Caption look: bold rounded font, white text with a dark outline, the current word highlighted in the accent colour.
- Colours: primary #FF6B35, accent #FFD23F, text #FFFFFF (replace with the brand values).
- Font: Be Vietnam Pro.
- Logo: small watermark bottom right for the whole video.
- Logo file: put it in `reference/` (PNG, JPG or WebP) and name it here, e.g. `reference/logo.png`.
- Music: light, upbeat lo-fi or acoustic, quiet under speech, a little louder in b-roll.
- Open with a hook in the first 3 seconds, end with a clear call to follow or comment.
- Avoid: long static shots, heavy colour grading, tiny captions, music that fights the voice.

## Reference usage

State what each file in `reference/` is for. Without a note the app guesses from its content.

- `sample1.mp4`: copy the caption style only.
- `track.mp3` is music, not narration.
- `intro.mp4`: copy the opening hook structure, do not use its footage or audio.
- `notes.txt` is the script outline; the order of topics matters, exact wording does not.

Files marked "ignore" are never used.
