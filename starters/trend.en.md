# Guideline: trend / short-form

## Brand style

- Tone: punchy, energetic, high contrast. Every second must earn its place.
- Pacing: shots of 0.7-1.5 seconds, cutting on the beat of the music.
- Transitions: hard cuts on the beat; quick zoom punches, flashes or glitches on big hits. Keep them short.
- Captions: centred or upper-middle, very large, one to three words at a time, popping in with a quick scale animation.
- Caption look: heavy bold font, uppercase allowed, thick outline, key words in the accent colour.
- Colours: primary #111111, accent #FF2D55, text #FFFFFF (replace with the brand values).
- Font: Be Vietnam Pro.
- Logo: small, top corner, visible from the start so it survives re-posting.
- Music: trending, strong beat, loud; the beat drives the edit. Drop in on the first beat.
- Hook in the first second, then a payoff at the end that invites a loop.
- Avoid: slow intros, long shots, small or thin captions, off-beat cuts, more than three words of caption at once.

## Reference usage

State what each file in `reference/` is for. Without a note the app guesses from its content.

- `sample1.mp4`: copy the caption style and the cutting rhythm, not the audio.
- `track.mp3` is music, not narration. Cut to its beats.
- `sample2.mp4`: copy the transitions only.

Files marked "ignore" are never used.
