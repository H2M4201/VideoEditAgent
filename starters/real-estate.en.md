# Guideline: real estate

## Brand style

- Tone: calm, elegant, trustworthy. Let the space speak; no hype.
- Pacing: shots of 3-5 seconds, slow and steady. Hold wide shots of rooms a little longer.
- Transitions: soft fades or dissolves only. No whips, zooms or glitch effects.
- Captions: bottom third, medium size, appear with a gentle fade (line by line, not word by word).
- Caption look: clean sans-serif, light weight, no outline, subtle shadow for legibility.
- Colours: primary #1F3A5F, accent #C9A24B, text #FFFFFF (replace with the brand values).
- Font: Be Vietnam Pro.
- Logo: small, top right, fixed for the whole video; show a larger end card in the last 2 seconds.
- Music: soft piano or ambient, slow tempo, low in the mix under narration.
- Lead with the best exterior or view, finish with price or contact details.
- Avoid: shaky footage, fast cuts, loud music, flashy effects, crowded text, tilted horizons.

## Reference usage

State what each file in `reference/` is for. Without a note the app guesses from its content.

- `sample1.mp4`: copy the caption style and pacing only, not the audio.
- `track.mp3` is music, not narration.
- `voice.wav` is the narration; build the timing around it.
- `script.txt` is the script; keep the wording as written.

Files marked "ignore" are never used.
